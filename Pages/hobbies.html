My Hobbies

Fountain Pens and Writing

I am an avid collector of fountain pens and use them in my daily life for taking notes, journaling, making lists, planning (I always keep a planner on me), and many more writing purposes. In addition, I also collect fountain pen inks as well to complement the many pens I have. My collection is ever growing.

I have been playing music since 13, and it is something that has always given me great joy to play on my guitar (own both acoustic and electric) as well as singing out to the songs I love listening to.

Hiking!

Hiking and long-distance walking is how I get a bulk of my exercise! I love taking hikes on different trails with my dog Aura and explore nature.

Daily Wordle

I am an avid player of the daily wordle and used to have a 75-day streak that I unfortunately lost to the word VIVID.
